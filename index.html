About Us

Trusted Printing Machinery Experts

Established in 2014, K.S Machinery in Tilak Road, Akola is a top player in the Printing Press category. We have established a firm foothold in the industry and act as a one-stop destination servicing customers both local and from other parts of Akola.

Our belief that customer satisfaction is as important as our products and services has helped us garner a vast base of customers. We specialize in Flex Printing Services, Digital Flex Printing, Screen Printing, Offset Printing, Invitation Card Printing, Sticker Printing, and all types of printing services.
